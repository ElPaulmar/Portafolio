My Story

Since childhood, video games have been more than entertainment to me: they are windows to incredible worlds, complex narratives and experiences that mark you. This fascination led me to study Digital Game Design at Universidad Andrés Bello, where I discovered my true passion: creating experiences that combine deep narratives with innovative mechanics.

Throughout my education, I have worked on various projects ranging from hack & slash with complex combat systems to visual novels with branching narratives. Each project has been an opportunity to learn something new about game design, interactive narrative and the importance of iteration in the creative process.

My approach always seeks integration: that mechanics reinforce the narrative and vice versa. I firmly believe that the best games are those where gameplay and story work together to create a cohesive and memorable experience.

My Design Philosophy

Narrative-Mechanic Integration

I believe the best gaming experiences emerge when narrative and mechanics reinforce each other, creating a cohesive experience.

Constant Iteration

Game design is an iterative process. Every test, every feedback, every mistake is an opportunity to improve and refine the experience.

Player-Centered

I design thinking about the player's experience, making sure every design decision serves to create memorable and meaningful moments.

Purposeful Narrative

Every story must have something to say. I focus on creating narratives that explore deep themes and connect emotionally with players.
