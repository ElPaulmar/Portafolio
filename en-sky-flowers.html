Sky Flowers - Project Breakdown

Game Designer Level Designer

Sky Flowers is a platforming game combining 2D and 3D that explores player greed. It is inspired by the Atacama desert bloom in northern Chile.

Summary

Sky Flowers is a platformer focused on exploration and on mechanically expressing player greed. The player must climb a giant stone figure while collecting flowers with a net, becoming heavier the more flowers are gathered.

This game was made as part of the Latin American Game Jam under the theme "The one who reaches for too much, grasps too little."

Team

The team consisted of 5 members: 2 people working on 2D art, 1 programmer, 1 audio designer, and myself handling mechanical design, level design, and asset implementation.

My Role

As the designer in a project that had to be completed in 9 days, leading the brainstorming process was essential to shape a solid game idea as quickly as possible. I introduced the Design Pillars method to a team that had never used it before, which helped us define the full game concept in under 12 hours.

After the initial concept work, my responsibilities shifted toward level design, focusing on creating and ideating the spaces the player would navigate, while also implementing the assets completed by the art team.

Project Goal

As this was a themed game jam project, the main goal was to represent the phrase "The one who reaches for too much, grasps too little" in a creative and entertaining way. To achieve that, we focused on bringing the theme through the player's collection mechanic by pushing the player toward greed.

Design

  • Core Systems: Aerial exploration, seed collection, and zone restoration.
  • Progression: Unlock routes and tools based on restoration milestones.
  • Narrative: Story told through environments, objects, and short dialogue moments.
  • Visual Direction: Soft palette, bright contrasts, and organic silhouettes.
  • Prototyping: Rapid iterations to validate pacing and objective clarity.
