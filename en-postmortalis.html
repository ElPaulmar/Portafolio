Postmortalis - Project Breakdown

Game Designer Writer Documentation

An innovative visual novel about the value that life can have at the end of the world. The game combines deep narrative choices with unique autopsy and investigation mechanics.

Summary

  • Context: Postmortalis was developed between February and December 2025 as an experimental visual novel centered on choices and emotional consequences.
  • Narrative Focus: The story explores the value of life in a collapsing world through investigations, autopsies, and narrative confrontations.
  • Method: Narrative and systems were iterated in parallel to keep tone, mechanics, and dramatic progression aligned.

Team

  • Team size: 6 people in core development.
  • Disciplines: Game Design, Programming, Art, Narrative, Audio, and QA.
  • Coordination: Regular sync meetings to review interactive narrative progress, systems design, and technical deliverables.

My Role

  • Game Design: Designed key systems: investigation, autopsies, and narrative decision structures.
  • Writing: Wrote characters, scenes, and branching narrative routes based on player decisions.
  • Documentation: Produced design documents, balance tables, and flow diagrams to align the team.

Project Goal

  • Experience Goal: Build an emotional experience where every decision has real narrative weight.
  • Design Goal: Integrate investigation systems with dialogue to strengthen immersion and player agency.
  • Production Goal: Deliver a solid narrative prototype with documented, scalable systems.

Design

  • Core Systems: Defined autopsy, investigation, and state-based dialogue routes.
  • Choice Structure: Designed branching paths with mechanical consequences and tone shifts.
  • Worldbuilding: Built post-apocalyptic context and characters with complex motivations.
  • Narrative Balance: Tuned rhythm between exposition, tension, and resolution in each chapter.
  • Technical Documentation: Prepared GDD, diagrams, and decision matrices for implementation.
