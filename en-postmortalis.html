Postmortalis - Project Breakdown

Game Designer Writer Documentation

An innovative visual novel about the value of life at the end of the world. The game combines deep narrative choices with unique autopsy and investigation mechanics.

Summary

  • Context: Postmortalis was a final-year university thesis project. Development was split into two stages: 5 months of pre-production, where the first design iteration and the initial GDD were built, followed by 5 more months of production to create the vertical slice.
  • Purpose: The vertical slice was meant to present the core existential narrative proposal, showing the range of characters, branching player choices, and the experimental mechanical concept.
  • Focus: The team used those two stages to refine the game design and validate the narrative and mechanical direction.

Team

  • Team size: 4 people: one programmer, one 2D artist, one 3D artist, and me as the designer.
  • Coordination: We met once a week to review progress and resolve any questions or needs from the team.
  • Production rhythm: As the final presentation approached, those meetings became more frequent.

My Role

  • Documentation: I focused on clearly documenting mechanics and systems for the programmer to keep production moving smoothly.
  • Iteration: I iterated on mechanics that were showing problems, both because of functionality and time limitations.
  • Narrative support: I also worked on all narrative-related tasks, including the implementation of a new tool for the team.

Project Goal

  • Narrative Goal: Convey the player experience of the project through a narrative-focused and experimental approach.
  • Vertical Slice Goal: Deliver a slice of at least 15 minutes that could show every key mechanic in the game.
  • Design Goal: Focus on the two main systems and create a mystery story that felt engaging but still easy to solve.

Design

  • Exploration Systems: This is where the player experience was shaped.
  • Autopsy Structure: This is where the player's work was centered.
  • Narrative: Because Postmortalis is a narrative game, story needed to carry real weight. Since the team was small, it was also important to work efficiently, so YarnSpinner was introduced as an essential production tool.
