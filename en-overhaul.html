Overhaul - Project Breakdown

Game Designer Writer

An innovative hack & slash that combines narrative with a unique Downgrades system where the player must strategically lose power, creating a cohesive narrative and mechanical experience.

Summary

  • Context: Overhaul was developed in 3 months as part of a university project.
  • Core Concept: The game proposes classic hack & slash progression with the added Downgrades mechanic, a change inspired by roguelike systems.
  • Meta Narrative: The project also includes a meta narrative where the game is aware that it is being played.

Team

  • Team size: 7 people: 3 2D artists, 1 3D artist, 2 programmers, and myself as the designer.
  • Coordination: Weekly reviews were used to align narrative and prioritize tasks.

My Role

  • Game Designer: Defined combat systems, progression rules, and the main Downgrades logic.
  • Narrative Designer: Wrote the main story, character arcs, and dialogue with gameplay impact.
  • Documentation: Consolidated design documentation (GDD, tables, and decision flow) to keep consistency across areas.

Project Goal

  • Experience Goal: Build a modular combat system that is deep, readable, and adaptable to different player styles.
  • Narrative Goal: Make the story justify every mechanical change, especially the progressive loss of abilities.
  • Production Goal: Deliver a solid vertical slice with a clear mechanical and narrative identity for academic evaluation and public showcase.

Design

  • Combat System: Designed weapon styles with differences in pace, range, and resource consumption.
  • Downgrades: Conceptually implemented inverse progression as the player’s mechanical and emotional axis.
  • Dialogue and Consequence: Structured conversations with decisions that change character perception and mission context.
  • Balancing: Defined damage tables and recovery windows to keep the challenge fair while power is reduced.
  • Combat UX: Adjusted visual feedback and state readability to improve clarity in intense fights.
