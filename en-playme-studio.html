PlayMe Studio - Project Breakdown

Game Designer QA Tester Documentation

A 490-hour professional internship at a game development studio. I worked as a Game Designer and QA Tester, contributing to the development and polish of multiple studio projects.

Summary

PlayMe Studio was a 490-hour professional internship in a real game production environment. The work combined QA, design, and documentation roles to support internal deliveries and continuous build improvement. I mainly worked on producing the team's first core documentation, helping improve production clarity and establish better design practices.

Team

During my internship period, PlayMe reached a total of 14 people working across design, programming, art, production, and QA.

The team coordinated through short meetings at the beginning of each workday, sharing task updates and milestone goals. I learned a lot from these dynamics and later prioritized applying them in future projects.

My Role

  • QA Tester: As QA, I focused on testing the game in development, identifying and documenting bugs. During my period at the studio, I identified, documented, and prioritized over 100 bugs.
  • Documentation: I generated technical documents for systems, levels, and implementation requirements.

Project Goal

Since this was a professional internship, my main objective was to learn as much as possible about the industry in a real work environment. I focused on learning QA tasks and the key foundations of good documentation a designer should know. That allowed me to integrate professional practices and cross-team communication with multidisciplinary teams.
